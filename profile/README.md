## 🌟 Key Features

- **Direct Integration**: Fill PDF forms directly from **Google Sheets**, **Excel**, or **CSV** files.
- **Visual Drag-and-Drop Mapper**: Pixel-perfect alignment for text, checkboxes, and signatures.
- **Privacy First**: All processing happens 100% in your browser. Your data is never uploaded to our servers.
- **Batch Processing**: Generate hundreds of personalized certificates, invoices, or contracts in seconds.
- **No Fillable PDF Required**: Use any standard PDF and overlay your data anywhere.

## 🛠️ How it Works

1. **Import Data**: Select your Google Sheet or upload an Excel/CSV file.
2. **Upload PDF**: Provide the PDF template you want to fill.
3. **Map Fields**: Use our visual editor to drag spreadsheet columns onto the PDF.
4. **Generate**: Preview and download your personalized PDFs as a single file or ZIP.
